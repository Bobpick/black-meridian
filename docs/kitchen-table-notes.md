# Kitchen-table notes

Record of the design conversation that shaped BLACK MERIDIAN after the first GitHub push. Family first. Small groups. This file is an asset to the household, not a war staff.

## What this game is for

- A grandfather’s table, four or five households, grandchildren who should live long enough to have grandchildren.
- Free. Not for sale.
- Named countries (United States, China, Taiwan, partners) as a **public shock frame**. Campaign events are invented. Not a forecast, not a targeting board, not a war plan.
- The player cannot command fleets. The player can keep water, medicine, a check-in, and a family.

## The hard wall (Kobayashi Maru)

The war is unwinnable from this table. Pass when the group can say: we cannot win the war, and we can still be a family on the worst night.

Unwinnable: stopping a great-power war, holding a border, guaranteeing the nightmare never happens.  
Winnable: check-in when apps fail; water and medicine; stay / move / share / wait said out loud; refusing to hunt a neighbor when a video says to.

## Hotspots

| Radio | What it is |
|---|---|
| Taiwan Strait | Most-discussed high-end shock (chips, shipping, fuel, rumor) |
| Canada / northern trade | Neighbor shock: power, heat, freight — not a war with Ottawa |
| Mexico / southern trade | Neighbor shock: food, fuel, freight — not a war with Mexico City |
| Homeland worst night | Nightmare as **effects**: jammed lines, ugly phones, late medicine, rumor. Canada and Mexico are congestion and story, not invasion routes. No landing maps. No armies named by race or religion. |

Do not encode ethnic or religious “invasion forces.” Unsafe neighbors are **behavior** (crime, threats), not a people-category. Then: second roof and official help — never a posse.

## Default course besides a hole in the ground

Stay in a known place unless the house itself fails (evac order, fire, flood, freeze/heat that will kill, medical need). Move once, to a named yes. Share without going to zero. Wait out rumor. Official alerts beat the loudest cousin.

## Radio mile markers (W-BM Family Net)

After each inject: world bulletin, then whether **this table’s** plan held, mixed, or failed — scored from the household form, not from hope. Untested comms copy late.

## Reality on the planner (what the conversation added)

**Food**

- Days on the shelf can run to two years. Bags already preserved count. Freeze-dryers need power. Sun/car/screens can dehydrate with no grid if the sky is clear. Storm, freeze, and smoke block sun-drying. Dried food still needs water. Do not advertise a long stock.
- Seeds are a months play, not 72-hour dinner.
- Wild protein: rabbit, squirrel, groundhog, fish, game birds if **practiced**. Crow/vulture are scavengers — last resort, not chicken. Not a hunting guide. Follow the law.

**Water**

- Stored gallons first.
- PA-style 90°F / high humidity: solar Peltier can recover **real** water from air (cups-to-liters, not a hydrant). Heat is still the killer. Arid/storm/freeze/short days block it.
- PA springs and PNW rivers that run through a dry spell count if you can get there and you **treat**. Seasonal creeks in a drought do not.
- Hydraulic ram pump: a **survival path**. No grid; needs flow and drop from a spring or perennial stream. Freeze or a dead creek stops it. On the Home-front session as **Grid-down water**: source → ram or gravity → treat → store.
- Sawyer-class hollow-fiber can treat a very large gallon count (maker ratings on the order of 100,000 gallons — follow the maker, backflush). Not chemical-proof.

**Heat to cook / boil**

- Solar cooker when the sun is out.
- Rocket stove with little fuel when it is not.
- Grid-only kitchens fail the mile.

**Weather and medicine**

- Status only: refill risk, power-device, heat/freeze/storm/smoke. No diagnoses, no drug names, no addresses.
- NOAA weather radio, shortwave receive, ham receive or licensed net, counted batteries or solar/crank, practiced at the check-in hour. Internet is not a radio. Receive first. Do not jam.

**People**

- Unprepared families are a **drain**, not automatically a violent threat.
- Sharing floor: keep X days, then help.
- Help kind: teach filter/boil/rocket; maybe one day of water once. Open pantry and stash tours fail the mile.

## 30-day grid-down “survival rate”

There is no honest statewide percentage. Oregon is about 4.2 million people; Pennsylvania about 13 million. Thirty days is mostly a water / medicine / heat-or-freeze / sanitation event, not a famine for healthy adults. A prepared house should plan to live. An unprepared house: many still live; deaths cluster among medically dependent and temperature-exposed people. This file cannot save a state. It can make **this** family one of the houses still standing on day 31.

## What this repository will not become

- Landing beaches, real bases, plants, or hospitals as targets
- People-hunts or ideology labels on the roster
- A fake survival score for a million strangers
- A hunting, trapping, or attack manual
